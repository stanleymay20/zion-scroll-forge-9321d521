## Issue

- Placeholder registration address `1234 Kingdom Way`, claimed Delaware registration, pending EIN treated as identity data, fixed 'Established' year;
- A purported founder/chancellor with degrees, LinkedIn URL and a named advisory body without verified appointment/credential documents;
- Specific named accreditors, dated candidacy/accreditation milestones without verification;
- Guarantee-like benefits (certificates for all courses, blockchain credentials, mentors), refund windows and transcript turnaround times without operational evidence;
- Inert "Read Full Policy", "Contact Us" and "View Sample Courses" buttons, and missing policy routes.

- Route privacy and academic-integrity links to existing public pages.
- Do not pretend to publish non-existent refund, appeals or transcript policies.
- Replace catalogue CTA with real `/catalog` link; disable contact until a verified support address is confirmed.
- Add browser regression coverage for claim removal and navigational behavior.
